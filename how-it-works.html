How it works

From a line in your notes to a launched product.

Seven steps. You can stop at any one of them, but every step gets your idea closer to something real, and every contribution gets the right person credit.

  1. 1
    The idea

    Post an idea

    Write it in a few lines: the problem, who it's for, what you'd build. No pitch deck needed.

    • Public: anyone on SAEDI can read it and join the discussion.
    • Private: only the people you invite can see it.
  2. 2
    The idea

    Discuss it with the community

    People ask questions, point out gaps and say whether they'd use it. You find out early if your idea has legs.

    Every question stays attached to the idea, so nothing gets lost in a group chat.

  3. 3
    The room

    Convert it into a room

    When an idea gains traction, the creator turns it into a room: a team space where it actually gets built.

    The discussion comes along, so the team starts with context instead of a blank page.

  4. 4
    The room

    Set roles and tasks

    List the roles you need and break the work into tasks people can pick up.

    Developers Designers Marketers Writers
  5. 5
    The room

    Log work. The team approves it.

    Every contribution is logged in the room. The team reviews it and approves what was done.

    Approved work goes on the record with your name on it. No more "who actually did this?"

  6. 6
    The credit

    Get a verified contribution profile

    Your approved work shows up on your profile: what you did, on which project, verified by the team.

    Share one link on your resume, on LinkedIn or in an interview.

  7. 7
    The credit

    Get rewarded if it launches

    If the project becomes a real product, contributors are rewarded according to their contribution.

    Your verified record is what counts, so every approved task matters.
