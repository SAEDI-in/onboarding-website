How it works

From a line in your notes to a team that builds it.

The full journey
  1. Step 1

    Post

    Share your idea publicly, or keep it private and invite only the people you trust.

  2. Step 2

    Discuss

    People ask questions, point out gaps and tell you if they'd use it.

  3. Step 3

    Open a room

    When an idea gains traction, turn it into a team space with roles and tasks.

  4. Step 4

    Build & get credit

    Every contribution is logged and verified by the team. It's yours to show.
